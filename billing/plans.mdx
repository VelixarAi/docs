Cortex prices are plus applicable taxes, calculated at checkout. The Cortex annual plan is $190/yr + tax, which is two months free compared with 12 × $19.

Limits are **monthly counters**, not storage capacity. They reset on the 1st of each month at 00:00 UTC. See [Usage limits](/billing/usage-limits).

## Free

For prototyping and evaluation:
- 100 memory stores and 500 retrievals per month
- 30 requests per minute
- No overage. Requests are rejected once a monthly limit is reached, until it resets on the 1st (UTC)

## Paid plans

On Cortex, Synapse and Hivemind you are not hard-blocked at the monthly limit. Usage past the limit is billed as overage. See [Overages](/billing/overages).

Hivemind limits are per seat: your plan's limit is the per-seat figure multiplied by your number of seats.

## Earlier plan names

Subscribers on earlier plans (Pro, Power, Team, Teams, Max) keep their plan and limits until they choose to change it. Pro, Power and Team correspond to Cortex, Synapse and Hivemind.

Upgrade or manage your plan from [Settings](https://velixarai.com/settings).
