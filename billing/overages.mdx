## How overages work

On paid plans (Cortex, Synapse, Hivemind) you are not hard-blocked at your monthly limit. Memory stores and retrievals past the limit are billed as overage through Stripe metered billing, on the invoice for that month.

On Free there is no overage. Requests are rejected at the limit until it resets on the 1st (UTC).

## Overage rates

Current overage rates are shown on [velixarai.com/pricing](https://velixarai.com/pricing) and at checkout. We do not repeat the figures here, so that this page cannot disagree with what you are actually charged.

## Monitoring

Track your usage in [Settings](https://velixarai.com/settings) or with `GET /v1/usage`. See [Usage limits](/billing/usage-limits).

<Tip>
  If you are regularly over your limit, compare the next plan up with your overage total. A higher plan is often cheaper.
</Tip>
